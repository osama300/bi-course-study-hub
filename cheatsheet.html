Data Fundamentals & DIKW أساسيات البيانات وهرم DIKW

Lecture 1–2

Structured vs. Semi-Structured vs. Unstructured Data (بيانات منظمة / شبه منظمة / غير منظمة)

TypeDefinitionTypical SourceExample
Structuredمنظمة Fixed, predefined schema — rows & columns with set data types Relational databases, Excel, ERP/CRM systems A Customers table with ID, Name, Email columns
Semi-Structuredشبه منظمة Some organizational tags/markers, but no rigid fixed schema — flexible fields APIs, NoSQL stores, log files, IoT feeds JSON, XML, an email (structured headers + free-text body)
Unstructuredغير منظمة No predefined format or internal organization at all Documents, multimedia, social media, sensors Images, videos, PDFs, free-text posts, audio

Which type has a predefined schema? → Structured. This exact question appeared in a real archived exam.أي نوع بيانات له مخطط محدد مسبقًا؟ ← منظمة. هذا السؤال بالضبط ظهر بأرشيف حقيقي.

DIKW Hierarchy (هرم البيانات-المعلومات-المعرفة-الحكمة)

LevelDefinitionExample
DataبياناتRaw facts, no context"120"
InformationمعلوماتOrganized data with context"120 units sold in October"
KnowledgeمعرفةActionable insight drawn from information"Sales rise every Q4 due to holiday demand"
WisdomحكمةIntegrated understanding of implications, used to act"Increase October inventory by 20% ahead of the surge"

OLTP vs. OLAP (معاملاتي مقابل تحليلي)

OLTP (Transactional)OLAP (Analytical)
PurposeDay-to-day operations — e.g. update a product priceMulti-dimensional analysis — e.g. top 10 profitable products
Size & SpeedSmall, very fastLarger (history + aggregation), speed depends on volume
UpdatesFast, simple inserts/updates by end usersMostly reads; periodic batch refreshes

KPIs & Balanced Scorecard المؤشرات وبطاقة الأداء المتوازن

Lecture 3–4

Types of KPI (أنواع المؤشرات)

TypeMeaningExample
StrategicاستراتيجيLong-term, high-level, shows if strategy is on targetOverall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)
OperationalتشغيليDetailed, timely, for daily decisionsDays Sales Outstanding (DSO)
LeadingاستباقيPredicts/influences future performanceMore maintenance checks → fewer defects
Laggingتابع (نتائجي)Shows the result of past performanceRevenue this month, units produced

Leading informs future action; Lagging validates past outcomes.الاستباقي يوجّه المستقبل، والتابع يُثبت نتائج الماضي.

SMART Criteria (معايير SMART)

  • Specific — clear, detailed description محدد
  • Measurable — quantifiable via numbers/%/amounts قابل للقياس
  • Achievable — ambitious yet attainable, no burnout قابل للتحقيق
  • Relevant — aligned with strategic objectives ذو صلة
  • Time-bound — realistic timeframe (quarter/month/year) محدد بزمن

Four BSC Perspectives + Cause-and-Effect Chain (أبعاد BSC الأربعة وسلسلة السبب والنتيجة)

PerspectiveKey Question
FinancialالماليHow do we look to shareholders?
CustomerالعملاءHow do customers see us?
Internal Processالعمليات الداخليةWhat must we excel at?
Learning & Growthالتعلم والنموCan we continue to improve?

Chain order (bottom-up): Learning & Growth → Internal Processes → Customer Satisfaction → Financial Results.الترتيب من الأسفل للأعلى: التعلم والنمو ← العمليات الداخلية ← رضا العملاء ← النتائج المالية.

Most Important KPI per Perspective (per practice ranking) (الأهم عمليًا بكل بُعد)

  • Financial: Revenue Growth & Net Profit Margin
  • Customer: Customer Retention Rate
  • Internal Process: On-time Delivery
  • Learning & Growth: Employee Retention Rate

BSC Implementation — 4 Phases (مراحل تطبيق BSC الأربع)

  1. Strategic Foundation Development بناء الأساس الاستراتيجي
  2. Scorecard Design & Metric Selection تصميم البطاقة واختيار المقاييس
  3. Organizational Alignment & Cascading المواءمة التنظيمية والتدرج
  4. Continuous Improvement & Evolution التحسين المستمر والتطور

Her real exam phrases this as: first phase = "clarifying & translating vision/strategy"; "cascading" = aligning scorecards across levels; "continuous improvement" = reviewing measures regularly.صياغتها بالاختبار الحقيقي: المرحلة الأولى = "توضيح وترجمة الرؤية والاستراتيجية"؛ cascading = مواءمة البطاقات بمختلف المستويات؛ التحسين المستمر = مراجعة المقاييس بانتظام.

Strategy Map (خريطة الاستراتيجية)

A visual representation showing cause-and-effect relationships between strategic objectives across all four BSC perspectives.تمثيل مرئي يوضح علاقات السبب والنتيجة بين الأهداف الاستراتيجية عبر الأبعاد الأربعة.

Conceptual vs. Logical vs. Physical (المستويات الثلاثة لنموذج البيانات)

LevelPurposeAudienceExample
ConceptualمفاهيميHigh-level business view of what the system containsBusiness stakeholders & executivesER / EER models
LogicalمنطقيHow the system is structured, independent of any DBMSComputer specialists & BI developersCustomer(ID, Name) 1:N Sales_Fact

Fact Table vs. Dimension Table (جدول الحقائق مقابل جدول الأبعاد)

Fact TableDimension Table
RepresentsA business event/measurement, sits at centerDescriptive context: Who? What? Where? When? How?
ContainsForeign keys + numeric measuresA (often surrogate) primary key + descriptive attributes

Additive vs. Semi-Additive vs. Non-Additive Measures (أنواع القياسات)

TypeRuleExample
AdditiveجامعةSummed across all dimensionsSales_Amount
Semi-Additiveشبه جامعةSummed across some dimensions, but not timeAccount_Balance
Non-Additiveغير جامعةCannot be summed — use averages/ratiosProfit_Margin_Percentage

Star Schema vs. Snowflake Schema (مخطط النجمة مقابل الثلج)

StarSnowflake
StructureFact table center, dimensions radiate directly as "points"Dimensions further normalized into sub-tables
Trade-offSimpler, faster queriesMore normalized, more storage-efficient, richer dimension detail
Use CaseGeneral analyticse.g. dealers across many countries/regions needing detail

5 Steps to Build a Dimensional Model (خطوات بناء النموذج البُعدي)

  1. Identify Business Process تحديد العملية التجارية
  2. Determine the Grain (lowest level of detail) تحديد الدقة التفصيلية
  3. Identify Facts تحديد الحقائق
  4. Identify Dimensions تحديد الأبعاد
  5. Build Schema (Star or Snowflake) بناء المخطط

Transactional (OLTP) vs. Analytical Database (تشغيلية مقابل تحليلية)

OLTPAnalytical DB
PurposeDay-to-day operations; fast transactions, many usersOrganize lots of stable data for easy analysis/retrieval

Data Warehouse vs. Data Mart vs. Data Lake (مستودع مقابل متجر مقابل بحيرة)

Data WarehouseData MartData Lake
ScopeEnterprise-wideOne departmentAny raw source, enterprise-wide
Size100GB–1TB+<100GBVariable, often huge
SchemaRequired (subject-oriented, integrated)Required, departmental scopeNo schema required upfront
Data FormatStructured, processedStructured, processedNative/raw, multi-structured (IoT, tweets, email, operational)

A Data Lake and a Data Warehouse can co-exist in the same architecture — one doesn't replace the other.يمكن لبحيرة البيانات والمستودع أن يتعايشا بنفس البنية — لا يستبدل أحدهما الآخر.

BI Delivery: Reports & Dashboards توصيل BI: التقارير واللوحات

Lecture 9–10

Report vs. Dashboard (التقرير مقابل اللوحة)

ReportDashboard
FocusDetailed fact data, tabular, numbers & textKey measures/KPIs, centralized, visual
AudiencePeople who need the data itselfPeople who need a quick glance/interpretation
PurposePrinting (styled) or exporting (raw)Single-screen monitoring

The Dashboard Formula (معادلة اللوحة)

Dashboard = Data/Information + Visual + User Interface. Data/Information is the most important element; Visual gives an at-a-glance view; UI unifies everything cleanly.اللوحة = البيانات/المعلومات + التصور + واجهة المستخدم. البيانات هي الأهم؛ التصور يعطي نظرة سريعة؛ الواجهة توحّد كل شيء.

Dashboard Levels (مستويات اللوحات)

LevelFocusExample
OperationalتشغيليةReal-time monitoring, a few key indicatorsCall center / reservation status
Tactical (Analytical)تكتيكية/تحليليةSeeking & navigating data to support analysisComparison, history, forecast, benchmarking
Strategic (Executive)استراتيجية/تنفيذيةHigh-level KPI summary, decision supportBuilt around a BSC or Strategy Map; bullet charts, gauges, traffic lights

Quick Glossary قاموس تعاريف سريع

All lectures — the "define X in one sentence" questions

Business Intelligence

ذكاء الأعمال

Transforms raw data into useful information for strategic & operational decisions.

KPI

مؤشر الأداء الرئيسي

A critical, quantifiable indicator of goal achievement.

Semantic Layer

الطبقة الدلالية

Translates business terms into technical queries, so non-technical users can query data in plain business language.

Data Model

نموذج البيانات

A visual/logical representation of data: its types, attributes, relationships, and how it's organized, stored & used.

Dimensional Model

النموذج البُعدي

A technique for analytical systems/warehouses, organizing data into Facts and Dimensions.

Grain

الدقة التفصيلية

The lowest level of detail captured in a fact table (e.g. one row per sales transaction line).

Data Cube

مكعب البيانات

A multidimensional structure (e.g. Product × Region × Time) that OLAP is optimized to query.

Managed Reporting

التقارير المُدارة

A technical developer prepares the data and builds the output for non-technical users — not automatic, not real-time.

Strategy Map

خريطة الاستراتيجية

A visual showing cause-and-effect across all four BSC perspectives.

Data Governance

حوكمة البيانات

Policies for data ownership, security, quality, metadata & master data.

ETL

الاستخراج والتحويل والتحميل

Extract (pull from sources) → Transform (clean/standardize) → Load (move into the warehouse).

Decision Support System (DSS)

نظام دعم القرار

A system where decisions are built directly on data, bridging the gap between data and actionable knowledge.
